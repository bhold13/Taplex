# TapLex Brand Notes

## Product Signal

TapLex should feel trustworthy, modern, professional, and warm. The interface should make networking feel organized and safe instead of noisy.

## Visual System

- Primary ink: `#121417`
- Primary teal: `#0f766e`
- Coral accent: `#ef7c5c`
- Lime signal: `#d6f46a`
- Sky support: `#cfe8ff`
- Warm background: `#f7f4ed`

## UI Principles

- Keep workflows dense enough for repeated daily use.
- Avoid oversized marketing surfaces inside operational pages.
- Use explainable AI language whenever recommendations or feed ranking are shown.
- Treat privacy and trust as first-class product states, not legal footnotes.
